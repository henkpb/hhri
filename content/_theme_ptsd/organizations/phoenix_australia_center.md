Promotes recovery for the 15 million Australians affected by trauma. We do this by working with individuals, organizations and the community to understand, prevent, and recover from the potential adverse effects of trauma. Phoenix Australia is an independent, not-for-profit organization with an affiliation with the University of Melbourne.
