This practical guide aims at providing torture rehabilitation centres and other NGOs involved in anti-torture activities with easily accessible and practical advice on how to most effectively engage with and contributeto country visits by the United Nations Subcommittee on Prevention of Torture(SPT) to promote relevant and high quality outcomes from the visit.
